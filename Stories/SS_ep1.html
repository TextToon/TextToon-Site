Episode 1: Awakening of the Forgotten

The world had changed.

A hundred years ago, the skies split open, and from the rift, an energy called Eldros flooded the world. Humanity evolved. Some awakened supernatural abilities, while others remained ordinary. Soon, cities turned into battlegrounds where powerful squads formed, fighting for dominance, survival, and purpose.

Among these warriors was Raine Ardent, a once-ordinary young man with a past he couldn’t remember. He was a rookie in Squad Revenant, a small but deadly group of fighters barely holding onto their reputation.

The Battlefield

“Raine, stay behind me!” yelled Aeris Valden, the fiery-haired second-in-command, as she raised a wall of flames to block an incoming ice spear. Their opponent, Squad Frostfang, was relentless, their leader, Kain Zerev, smirking as he crushed the ground beneath them with a frost-laden stomp.

“You’re in my way, Valden,” Kain sneered. “Why don’t you step aside before your rookie gets hurt?”

Raine clenched his fists, the Eldros energy within him thrumming violently. He hated feeling weak, useless. His squad fought fiercely, but Frostfang had the upper hand. The battle was turning into a massacre.

Zeke, Revenant’s swordsman, was locked in a brutal clash against Frostfang’s dual-wielding warrior, sparks flying as their blades clashed. Luna, their healer, struggled to keep up, her violet eyes filled with worry.

Aeris wiped blood from her lip, glaring at Kain. “I’d rather die than surrender.”

“Then die.”

A flash of ice shot forward—too fast. Too powerful.

Raine moved without thinking.

His body ignited—not with fire, but with a black and silver aura, tendrils of light bursting from his skin. A shockwave erupted, sending ice shards flying back toward Frostfang’s warriors.

Silence.

Everyone stared.

“What... was that?” Aeris gasped.

Raine panted, his vision spinning. Something inside him had awakened, something beyond Eldros. For a split second, he saw flashes—memories—his past, locked away.

And then, everything went dark.

The Awakening

When Raine woke, he was in Revenant’s base. A dimly lit underground hideout, walls lined with maps, weapons, and glowing Eldros crystals.

Aeris sat beside him, arms crossed.

“You passed out,” she said. “Care to explain what the hell that power was?”

“I... don’t know.” He clenched his fists. “I’ve never used Eldros like that before.”

Zeke leaned against the wall. “That wasn’t Eldros. It was something else.”

Aeris narrowed her eyes. “You better figure it out fast. Because whatever it was... it just made you the most wanted fighter in the city.”

Raine shivered. He could feel it—the shift in the air. This was only the beginning.

And something deep inside him whispered:

"Remember who you are."
